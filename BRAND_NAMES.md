# Brand research: the Syllentra direction

Selected brand: **Syllentra**, authorized on 5 October 2026. The app, window title, and packaging now use Syllentra. Existing model and preference storage retains its original identity. The research below records the options considered before selection.

Second-round research: 5 October 2026. This section supersedes the first-round recommendations archived below, following the user's preference for Syllentra's word style.

## Direction

Keep the flowing pronunciation, compact shape, and restrained technical character of Syllentra. The new names need not reuse its prefix or suffix. Prefer approximately seven to nine letters, a clear spoken rhythm, and a spelling that works in English and Spanish. The associations and suggested pronunciations below are design judgments, not established meanings or etymologies.

## Deeper check of Syllentra

No clear exact-name product match emerged, and `syllentra.com` returned HTTP 404 from Verisign's registry RDAP endpoint. Close-spelling checks did, however, find several software identities using **Silentra**:

- [Silentra Networks](https://www.silentranetworks.com/): network automation and testing, including AI-assisted tools.
- [Silentra](https://silentra.co/): a digital exposure and privacy protection platform.
- [Silentra](https://silentra.app/): secure private messaging.
- [SILENTRA Ticket](https://ticketbot.silentra.me/docs): a Discord ticket management bot.

Depending on pronunciation, Syllentra and Silentra could sound almost identical. That presents a practical distinction and spelling problem, rather than proving a legal conflict. Syllentra remains a useful style reference, but I would lower its ranking as a final name. The first-round exact-spelling screen did not catch these closer matches.

## New shortlist

| Name | Suggested pronunciation | Character | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lirisca** | lih-RISS-kah | Compact, fluid, precise; a subtle lyrical association. My preference on sound and shape. | Personal and fictional uses exist, plus a nearby GitHub repository spelling, Liriscat. |
| **Eloceris** | eh-loh-SEH-riss | Classical and articulate; suggests elocution and expression. Strongest connection to the product's purpose. | Four syllables despite only eight letters; English and Spanish can pronounce the soft C differently. |
| **Lenveris** | len-VEH-riss | Restrained, contemporary, professional; accommodates broader future features. | More abstract, and visually close to Linveris, a bedding product name. |
| **Eluntris** | eh-LOON-triss | Fluid opening and crisp ending; a slightly more technical character. | Could feel like a fantasy name if the visual identity reinforces that association. |
| **Relistra** | reh-LISS-trah | Suggests organizing words and records; a productivity application character. | Unrelated song and fictional-name uses make its search footprint less empty. |

For sound and appearance, start with **Lirisca** and **Lenveris**. For a stronger connection to spoken language, start with **Eloceris**. Eloceris and Eluntris had the least clutter in the checks performed.

Additional searches combined each exact name with app, software, company, brand, transcription, audio, and trademark terms. Domain-restricted searches checked indexed pages from GitHub, Apple, Google Play, PyPI, and npm. No exact-name app/package listing appeared.

Searches restricted to indexed trademark-related pages on EUIPO, WIPO, USPTO, the UK IPO, Justia Trademarks, and Trademarkia returned no exact-name match. This was a search-engine check, not a complete search inside each trademark registry.

Close spellings checked included Lyrisca/Lyriska/Lirisqa, Elociris/Elokeris/Eloqueris, Lenverys/Lenvaris/Leneveris, Eluntrys/Eluntra/Eluntis/Eluntrix, and Relystra/Relistre/Relisstra. No clear competing software brand emerged from these variants. This is a selected set, not an exhaustive phonetic search.

The direct lookups used public GitHub repository search, PyPI package JSON, npm registry search, Apple's Search API for the US and Spain, and `https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com`. App Store search returns approximate matches. GitHub repository search does not cover all users, organizations, code, or private projects. An absent PyPI package does not establish that its name can be claimed.

HTTP 404 from the domain registry means no registration record was returned at the time checked. It does not guarantee purchase availability, and no domain or handle was reserved. Trademark clearance and social-handle availability remain unverified.

- [Relistra](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/lofibeatz/1534585346) is a song title, and the spelling also appears as a [fictional character name](https://www.worldanvil.com/hero/699c66af-3086-4637-af7e-f2b26644f3f5).

Before adoption, check exact and similar names inside the relevant official trademark databases, including [WIPO](https://branddb.wipo.int/), [EUIPO](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/), and [OEPM](https://www.oepm.es/). This screening does not establish legal clearance or cover every jurisdiction and unindexed product.

# First-round archive

The recommendations below are historical and superseded by the second round above.

Research date: 5 October 2026. The product is a local speech transcription app that will support multiple recognition engines. The name should work as one word, fit a compact sidebar, and avoid tying the product to Python, Whisper, or a particular model.

## Recommended candidates

| Name | Pronunciation | Brand direction | Screening result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vocivell** | voh-see-vell | Suggests voice becoming a written page; a compact, professional wordmark. My first choice. | No clear indexed product, company, or app match found in the exact-name and product searches described below. |
| **Auraleno** | aw-ra-len-oh | Softer and broader, with an association to listening and audio. Suitable if the product later expands beyond transcription. | No clear indexed product, company, or app match found. |

These are coined brand suggestions. Their associations and ranking are design judgments, not claims about established word meanings.

## What was checked

- Exact quoted searches for each name.
- Each name combined with app, software, transcription, brand, company, and trademark terms.
- Searches restricted to GitHub, Apple's App Store, and Google Play. The combined exact-name searches returned no results.
- Verisign's .com RDAP endpoint for `vocivell.com`, `auraleno.com`, and `syllentra.com`: each returned HTTP 404, with no registration record returned at the time of the check. This is a registry lookup result, not a promise of purchase availability.

The search did not establish trademark clearance or cover every unindexed business, app, language, or jurisdiction. Before adopting a name, check the chosen spelling and close variants in the relevant trademark registries, including the [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/), and confirm the desired domain and account handles.
